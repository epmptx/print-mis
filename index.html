❓ Help & Reference

Pre-MIS — Job Intake
1. Sales Won → QuickBooks Estimate → Convert to Work Order
2. Email sent to CSR → Work Order printed
3. Job data entered in EPMP Job Status Sheet
4. Comment Card generated and printed
5. Plastic Job Jacket assembled (Work Order + Delivery Slip + Samples + Comment Card)
6. Job Jacket placed with Scheduling for MIS entry
MIS Production Flow
1. Scheduling opens ＋ New Job or uses 📥 Job Queue to pre-fill from the Job Status Sheet
2. Fill in Customer, Order In Date, Due Date, Description, Quantity and MR fields → Schedule This Job
3. Click 📋 Material Req to print or save the MR to Google Drive
4. Click ➕ Begin Next Operation → select Department, Machine, Operator → job moves to that dept
5. Department operators log Make Ready, Production, and Units as they work
6. When done, operator clicks ✓ Complete or ⏹ Stop Production → card shows Awaiting Next
7. Next department clicks ➕ Begin Next Operation to pull the job → repeat steps 5–7 per department
8. Shipping/Inventory clicks ✓ Mark Job Complete → job enters Archive after 3 days
Post-Production — Outside MIS
1. Delivery to customer
2. Invoice created in QuickBooks
3. Job Jacket broken down and filed
Key Rules
One card per job — a job exists in exactly one department at a time. Moving it removes it from the previous dept.

Jobs can skip departments — not every job goes through Folding, Bindery, Finishing, Mailing, and Fulfillment. Follow the Production Ticket specs.

Jobs can regress — if rework is needed, use Begin Next Operation to send a job back to any previous department.

Press counts press sheets — all other departments count ordered quantity. These are different numbers.
